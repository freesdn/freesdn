# Getting Support

FreeSDN is a single-maintainer open-source project. Here is where to go for
what you need.

## Documentation first

The complete manual lives at **[docs.freesdn.org](https://docs.freesdn.org)** —
installation, configuration, every module and adapter, the API reference, and
operational runbooks (backup/restore, upgrades, recovery). It is the canonical
source of truth and is more complete than anything in this repository.

## Questions and discussion

For "how do I…?", "is this supported?", and general discussion, use
**[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/freesdn/freesdn/discussions)**.
Please search first — common questions are often already answered.

## Bug reports

Found something broken? Open a
**[bug report](https://github.com/freesdn/freesdn/issues/new/choose)**. A good
report (version, module/adapter, steps to reproduce, expected vs actual, logs
with secrets redacted) gets fixed far faster. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for what makes a great report.

## Security issues

**Do not** open a public issue for a vulnerability. Use GitHub Private
Vulnerability Reporting or email **security@freesdn.org**. See
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What to expect

FreeSDN is maintained by its sole owner and developer. Community support depends
on maintainer capacity; this document does not promise a response time,
availability commitment, or enterprise support SLA. Issues and discussions are
read and triaged. Clear reproduction steps and redacted diagnostics help the
maintainer investigate problems.
